What goes into your estimate

Four input groups, one model

Travel

Personal vehicle, public transport, flights and train journeys — usually the single biggest lever.

Energy & water

Household electricity and water usage, converted into their share of daily emissions.

Diet & waste

Diet type and daily waste, which shift emissions more than most people expect.

How a prediction is generated

You submit habits

The form sends your eight inputs to the API as JSON.

Fields are validated

The server checks every field is present and non-negative.

Model scores it

A Random Forest regressor turns your inputs into a kg CO₂/day figure.

You get a read

The result comes back with a category, badge color and a tailored tip.
